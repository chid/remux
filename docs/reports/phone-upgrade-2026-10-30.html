What changed

Touch & layout

Controls with room to use them

Nine dock actions have distinct 44 × 44-point targets and wrap into two rows when needed. Save actions remain reachable above the keyboard; shortcut forms open at full height.

Writing & readability

A bounded, scrolling composer

Long drafts scroll, landscape growth caps at two lines, and larger text retains a complete line. A narrow synchronization fix prevents a demonstrated model-publication race from erasing typed characters and newlines.

Settings & recovery

State changes finish in order

Rapid settings edits persist in order with failure rollback. Home restores the prior window appearance. Trusting a discovery host retries immediately and rejects stale discovery results.

Sessions & navigation

Current focus and topology stay current

Herdr drops superseded queued focus moves. Native tmux window-close handling removes known topology once, ignores duplicates and unknown IDs, and emits no extra commands. Save/Return dismisses setup before navigation.

All five final SE phone acceptance cases passed: portrait/landscape, keyboard shown/hidden, exact multiline draft preservation, larger text, and session/window/pane sheet topology.

What still needs validation

  1. Duo scene rotation and backgrounding

    Three UI cases timed out waiting for actual landscape. The native scene stayed 466 × 678 points while XCTest reported landscape. Home also failed to background in an earlier live case; the alternate device-control path was unavailable.

  2. Keyboard continuity on composer close

    Duo observed one keyboard-will-hide notification where zero was expected. The final keyboard remained visible and terminal focus was restored, but those snapshots do not rule out a transient hide. The same unchanged continuity assertion passed on SE.

  3. Duo shortcut and browser interactions

    Key mode stayed on Ctrl after a tap. Safari exposed its native close control as Cancel rather than Done. The corrected browser selector and stronger Key-selection check passed on SE; the Duo follow-up did not reach them. Created-shortcut live execution is also unverified.

  4. Physical target and remaining reliability limits

    The separate physical Duo’s model, iOS version, and app viewport are unknown. An earlier cold-boot draft truncation and isolated SE long-press failure remain unexplained despite later passes. No physical Duo, WAN-loss, live Herdr, or performance claim is made.

All failed assertions remain enabled. The reviewed native artifact still requires asset promotion and a pin update before TestFlight; the distribution gate is unchanged.

Test commands and reproduction notes

Discover destinations before selecting a simulator. The retained run records contain exact UUIDs, per-run environment settings, artifact paths, and exit statuses.

xcodebuild -showdestinations -project Remux.xcodeproj -scheme Remux
xcodebuild test -project Remux.xcodeproj -scheme Remux   -destination 'platform=iOS Simulator,id=<discovered-uuid>'
xcodebuild test -project Remux.xcodeproj -scheme RemuxUIOnly   -destination 'platform=iOS Simulator,id=<discovered-uuid>'
xcodebuild build -project Remux.xcodeproj -scheme Remux   -configuration Release -destination 'generic/platform=iOS'
